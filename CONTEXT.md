# Demoloop glossary

## Audience

The people for whom a demonstration is made. An audience is distinct from the people or systems operating the product.

## Actor context

An evidence-backed operating context used during a demonstration, including its session or authorization requirements. Its identifier comes from the product; there is no fixed actor vocabulary.

## Capability

Something the product enables an audience or actor to accomplish or understand. A capability may be state-changing, read-only, exploratory, operational, or content-oriented.

## Journey

A coherent objective that connects actor contexts, capabilities, important states, transitions, outcomes, and proof surfaces.

## Important state

A product condition that matters to the demonstrated value, whether persisted, transient, computed, or observed.

## Transition

An evidence-backed causal change between important states. A transition may result from a user action, asynchronous work, another actor, or an external system.

## Observable outcome

The visible value produced or revealed by a journey. An outcome does not require a write operation; an insight or diagnostic result can be an outcome.

## Proof surface

A visible product surface that demonstrates an outcome or supports a product claim.

## Safe demonstration action

An action supported by evidence and safe to perform with the configured data and environment. Discovery does not assume that every capability has one.

## Scene purpose

The editorial function of a scene: hook, context, interaction, exploration, state-change, handoff, result, proof, montage, or close.

## Output

A planned public master, actor journey, feature clip, release demo, or montage. Coverage across outputs is separate from the quality of any one output.

## Technical quality

Evidence that execution, safety, artifacts, encoding, resolution, and audio mechanics satisfy their contracts.

## Editorial quality

Evidence that the video communicates value through a coherent narrative, readable visuals, directed attention, suitable pacing, clear transitions, an outcome, and—when required—a deliberate opening and close.

## Agent editorial review

A chronological inspection of the rendered MP4 performed after deterministic evaluation. It is external to the CLI runtime and cannot be claimed unless its report exists and validates.
